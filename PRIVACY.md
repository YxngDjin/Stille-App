# Privacy Policy — Stille

*Last updated: May 2026*

> 🌿 Stille does not collect any personal data. All your entries remain exclusively on your device.

Dennis Lehmann  
Germany

## 2. What data is stored?

Stille stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- Mood entries (mood, note, date)
- Highlights (text, date)
- App settings (reminder time, volume, etc.)

This data is **not** transmitted to servers, not stored in the cloud, and not shared with third parties.

## 3. No accounts, no cloud

Stille does not require registration or an account. No user data is stored on external servers.

## 4. In-App Purchases

Stille offers voluntary in-app purchases as donations. These are processed exclusively through Apple's App Store. Apple's privacy policy applies. Stille itself does not receive any payment data.

Apple's Privacy Policy: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 5. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Stille uses your iPhone's local notification system. No data is sent to external servers.

## 6. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications | For daily check-in reminders |
| File access | Only for manual import of backup files |

These permissions are optional and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Since Stille does not process personal data on external servers, most GDPR rights can be exercised directly on your device:

- **Data deletion:** Settings → Delete all data
- **Data export:** Settings → Export entries
- **Uninstall app:** Deletes all data completely

## 8. Children

Stille is not specifically directed at children under 13. No data from children is collected.

## 9. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be communicated in the app.
